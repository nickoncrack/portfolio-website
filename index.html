02 A LITTLE CONTEXT

Curiosity, with
a debugger.

I'm a computer engineering student drawn to the layers most people get to ignore.

I like taking systems apart conceptually, finding the boundary where the abstraction ends, and building something on the other side. That currently means custom architecture and OS development, compiler internals, Linux, and the maths behind physical simulations.

I don't need every project to become a product. Sometimes the point is to learn exactly why a thing works.
